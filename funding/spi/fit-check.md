## What SPI publishes

**Process and fit.** SPI starts with a non-committal board discussion, then a board sponsor, formal application and board vote. The application names the project, needed services, liaison and replacement process, and existing obligations. SPI assesses free-software community character, established development and release history, and audience or significance. Discussions normally precede a vote by at least three days; invitations usually allow 60 days to accept. There is no automatic acceptance. ([HOWTO](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/associated-project-howto/))

**Costs.** Joining and remaining associated is free. SPI deducts 5% of donations for overhead plus attributable transaction fees; expense-payment fees also come from project funds. ([HOWTO](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/associated-project-howto/))

**Services.** SPI holds earmarked donations and pays documented, liaison-approved expenses consistent with its purposes and donor restrictions. It does not fundraise for projects. Contracts require an SPI officer unless SPI expressly authorizes another signatory. Substantial assets purchased with SPI funds and valued over $300 belong to SPI. SPI provides accounting and public financial reports, which could replace duplicate administration here. ([Services](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/services/))

**Payments.** Recurring or large service payments need contracts; smaller services or reimbursements may use invoices. The FAQ requires advance contractor or participation agreements for payments to individuals as applicable; confirm how that applies to the specific reimbursement or service arrangement rather than assuming every volunteer is a contractor. Liaison validation and receipts are required; normal processing is NET-30 after a complete invoice. SPI publishes payment restrictions concerning sanctions, embargoed-country residence or citizenship, unreasonable amounts and private benefit. Ask SPI how its rules apply to each arrangement; do not infer clearance from a contributor's public profile. ([Treasurer's FAQ](https://www.spi-inc.org/treasurer/faq/))

**Donations.** SPI is a U.S. 501(c)(3); qualifying gifts can be deductible for U.S. taxpayers, with donor-specific limits. Its published methods include checks, card, PayPal, Zelle, arranged wires and in-kind gifts. Cryptocurrency is not listed. No omission proves prohibition or support: ask whether it could accept, hold or convert bitcoin. Direct gifts to the team's node do not become SPI donations. ([Donations](https://www.spi-inc.org/donations/), [HOWTO](https://www.spi-inc.org/projects/associated-project-howto/))

None of this approves OogaBoogaX, its AI expenses, direct bitcoin receipts or a BTC treasury. SPI's fund and asset rules may constrain transfers or holding arrangements; obtain written answers before selecting it for that purpose.

## Licensing

SPI's [1998 intellectual-property policy](https://www.spi-inc.org/corporate/resolutions/1998/1998-11-16.iwj.2/) governs IP it owns or manages. Software copyright assigned to SPI must be released GPL-compatibly; the page does not establish a blanket license rule for all associated-project code. Ask SPI how it would treat retained copyrights and the custom text. Do not promise a change while the team's license direction is **undecided**.

The [licensing brief](../../governance/licensing.md) owns the options and migration proposal. The contributor numbers above exclude identified automation but cannot establish who owns every contribution or whose permission a change requires.

## Other routes

The [funding plan](../README.md) contains the comparison and Proposed sequence, including bill reduction, direct support, grants and hosts. These two restrictions remain relevant:

- **Software Freedom Conservancy:** requires OSI-approved and DFSG-free software licensing. Projects developed for less than a year or only at proof-of-concept stage are generally ineligible. The earliest repository anniversary is August 25, 2027, calculated from August 25, 2026 + one year; that is a planning checkpoint, not proof of project age or future acceptance. ([Applying](https://sfconservancy.org/projects/apply/))
- **OpenSats:** its General Grant route funds qualifying public Bitcoin-related work with a proper open-source license. RED reimburses LLM tokens and related compute for concrete Bitcoin security red-teaming, not general maintenance or the team's broader AI budget. No application or award exists here. ([Apply](https://opensats.org/apply), [RED](https://opensats.org/apply/red))
