# Private AI cost worksheet

**Status:** blank template. **Proposed** preparation method; no costs, providers, approvals, or billing owners are asserted.

Copy this file to `private/ai-cost-worksheet-YYYY-MM.md` **before entering any real information**. Fill it in only under `private/`, with access restricted to the people doing the review. Keep invoices and account details in restricted storage; use private evidence references. Gitignore is a convenience, not access control. Never force-add the completed copy. Publish only category/project summaries in the [budget](../finance/budget.md), not the completed worksheet.

The columns match the budget's [line-item worksheet](../finance/budget.md#line-item-worksheet). Add a row per distinct bill or plan and reporting period. Do not duplicate one account across projects.

- **Ref:** a neutral record ID, not an invoice number, email, or account ID.
- **Monthly amount:** the full monthly planning cost before project allocation, with its currency and forecast period. For annual billing, divide by 12 only for planning; preserve the full invoice, service period and actual payment separately. This allocation is not evidence of monthly cash payments. Do not mix currencies without a dated conversion source.
- **Basis:** the estimate method and supporting historical records. Distinguish Actual invoiced/incurred from Actual paid, with service/payment periods and supporting evidence. An invoice alone does not prove payment. A projection from an actual invoice remains an Estimate for a future month.
- **Project share:** fraction and allocation method; list shares per project and keep their sum at or below the full cost. Project amount = monthly amount × attributable fraction. Do not treat mixed personal use as entirely project use.
- **Status:** Draft / Proposed or Approved, linking the approval reference if one exists. Actual cost evidence does not by itself mean reimbursement is approved.

Privately attach or reference the billing owner, currency, evidence, service and payment periods, renewal date, and any awarded credit's restrictions and expiry. For free access, record the recipient and cost actually displaced as a memo, not a cash receipt. Do not assume a personal benefit is a contribution to the team. Check overlapping subscriptions, duplicate claims and whether invoice amounts already include credits before totaling.

Publish a summary only after a second reviewer checks the arithmetic, project shares, and [savings and spending controls](../finance/budget.md#proposed-savings-and-spending-controls). Unknown amounts stay Unknown, not zero. Neither this template nor a filled copy approves an expense.
