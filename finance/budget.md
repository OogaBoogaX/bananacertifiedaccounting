# Project expense budget

**Status:** initial estimate; not approved and not an accounting ledger.

**Estimate supplied:** October 17, 2026.

**Planning currency:** USD, to confirm before a formal application.

## Known estimate

The team's estimated project-related AI costs are **$2,500–$5,000 per month**, with some months below or above that range. The estimate covers the team collectively; it is not a per-contributor allowance.

| Planning view | Amount | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Lower end of stated monthly range | $2,500 | Not an established minimum operating cost |
| Upper end of stated monthly range | $5,000 | Not an approved cap or guaranteed maximum |
| Annualized range | $30,000–$60,000 | Monthly range multiplied by 12; not a twelve-month forecast |

The annualized range excludes sponsor fees and other costs not yet quantified. No donation revenue, grant award, opening balance, or expense transaction is recorded by this estimate. Current personal outlays do not automatically become debts owed by a future sponsor or organization.
