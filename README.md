# Banana Certified Accounting 🍌🦍

Count bananas. Keep receipts. Build in public.

This is Team Ooga Booga's home for funding plans, project budgets, proposed expense rules, and public financial updates. We are a worldwide group of volunteer developers building freely available software and helping people learn to contribute.

Our immediate funding goal is to cover project expenses, primarily AI development tools. Paid development may be considered later if donations or grants support it.

## Current status

As of October 17, 2026, we are exploring fiscal sponsorship through Software in the Public Interest (SPI). The inquiry and expense policy are drafts. No sponsor acceptance or tax-deductible donation arrangement is established by these documents.

Estimated project-related AI costs are **$2,500–$5,000 per month**, with some months outside that range. These are planning estimates, not audited expenses, committed donations, or an approved spending allowance.

## How we use this repository

- Keep estimates, actual transactions, proposals, and approved decisions clearly labeled.
- Use pull requests to discuss changes to plans and policies.
- Record the date and evidence when a funding arrangement or policy is approved.
- Publish useful summaries; keep original invoices, tax forms, donor identities, bank details, and account credentials in restricted storage outside this public repository.

The [existing Ooga Booga License](LICENSE) remains in place. Its wording expresses our broad reuse intent; compatibility with a prospective sponsor will be reviewed during intake.
