# Decision log

The single place to record decisions about funding, budgets, policies, and outward communication. An absent entry means approval is **not established in these records**. Check supporting agreements and other evidence before concluding that no decision or obligation exists; this log does not create or extinguish liabilities.

## Proposed decision process

**Proposed, not adopted.** These review counts are recommendations, not existing authority. No one is appointed and no spending limit is approved by this text.

An **active maintainer** would mean someone the team recognizes as currently responsible for maintaining an affected project and available to review the decision. Before adopting this process, agree on that role's membership and record the adoption and roster reference. A contributor count does not establish voting rights.

| Decision | Proposed minimum review | Record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Routine documentation or factual correction with no new commitment | One active maintainer other than the author | Merged pull request; add a log entry only if it records a decision |
| Money, budget, policy adoption, roles, licensing, applications, donation instructions, or external commitments/communications | Two unconflicted active maintainers other than the proposer, after at least seven calendar days for affected maintainers to comment | Explicit approval of the final scope and terms, then a decision-log entry |

Notify affected maintainers when opening the pull request. Separate approval to draft from approval to send, publish, apply, or spend; name the exact action in the decision. Silence is not approval. A proposed license change also needs the rights review in the [licensing brief](licensing.md), and sponsor-held funds remain subject to the sponsor's authorization.

Disclose conflicts and recuse from the approval. If the proposed minimum cannot be met, or a material objection remains unresolved, keep the item Proposed and resolve the objection or propose a different process openly. Do not use a policy exception to bypass a conflict, missing approval, or sponsor rule. No monetary fast-track threshold is proposed before the budget is itemized.

For adoption of this process itself, seek the same two independent approvals and comment period, with the team first confirming who can act as an active maintainer. Record who agreed and how. Revisit the process after the first three months of actual use, or when a host imposes different requirements.

## How to record a decision

Add a row through a pull request. Keep evidence private if needed, and point to it with a reference rather than publishing it.

- **Date:** when the decision was made, not when it was written down.
- **Decision:** what was decided, in one or two sentences.
- **Decided by:** who agreed, by role or public handle, and how (for example, unanimous among active maintainers, or sponsor approval).
- **Evidence:** a public link (pull request, issue, published reply), or a private-record reference ID.
- **Affects:** the documents that should be updated as a result.

Examples of what belongs here: sending the SPI inquiry, a sponsor's response, adopting the expense policy, appointing a liaison or reviewer, approving a budget, changing the license, and publishing donation instructions.
