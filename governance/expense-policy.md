# Project expense policy

**Status:** proposal for team and prospective sponsor review.

**Prepared:** October 17, 2026.

**Effective date / approving decision:** not adopted.

This proposal describes how project funds could be managed. It does not authorize spending or promise reimbursement. An accepted sponsor's agreement and expense rules would govern funds it holds.

## Purpose and scope

Initially, funds would support documented expenses for approved public projects, primarily AI development tools. Salaries, contractor compensation, unrestricted contributor allowances, and distribution of surplus are outside this initial expense-only proposal. Future paid work would require a separate decision and appropriate arrangements.

## Roles to appoint

- **Project liaison:** communicates with the sponsor and maintains the current scope and budget.
- **Records coordinator:** keeps invoices, approvals, and payment records in restricted storage and prepares public summaries.
- **Expense reviewer:** checks project purpose, available funds, and supporting records. A person seeking reimbursement does not approve their own claim; use an uninvolved reviewer.

These are proposed responsibilities, not appointments. The team may combine administrative roles if independent review is preserved and the sponsor permits it.

## Spending process

1. Identify the project purpose, provider, expected amount, and any recurring commitment.
2. Obtain approval under the agreed budget and the sponsor's rules before committing funds. Team members must not bind a sponsor to contracts without its authorization.
3. Prefer project-specific billing where practical. For mixed use, document a reasonable project allocation for review rather than charging the entire personal bill automatically.
4. Retain the invoice, date, amount, currency, project allocation, and approval record. Use a public reference ID if a summary needs to refer to private evidence.
5. Request direct payment or reimbursement through the approved channel. Do not assume past personal expenses are reimbursable.
6. Record payment only when confirmed, and reconcile records before publishing totals.

## Variable AI usage

Agree on a monthly budget and usage limits before funding recurring services. Seek additional approval for costs outside those limits. Unspent funds stay available for the approved project purpose; they are not automatically paid out to contributors.

## Transparency

Publish aggregate monthly income, expenses, available funds, and completed work when the records are sufficient. Identify estimates and unresolved reconciliation items. Keep donor identities, original receipts, tax forms, banking details, credentials, and private correspondence outside this repository.

Review this policy if a sponsor is accepted, project scope changes, or paid development is proposed.
