# Team Ooga Booga 🪨🍌🦍

We eat bananas. We make code. We give code to the tribe.

Team Ooga Booga is a worldwide community of volunteer developers building free, publicly available software and helping more people learn to contribute. We use AI tools as part of our development process and share what we learn in public.

We call it **gorilla open source**: bring an idea, build in public, and help the next person get started.

[Visit our homepage](https://oogabooga.rocks)

## Our caves

- **[EntropyLab](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/entropylab)** — An offline Bitcoin key and wallet calculator for exploration, testing, and learning.
- **[Lightning Foundry](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/lightningfoundry)** — An early-stage platform for operating, automating, and studying Lightning routing nodes, including routing economics.
- **[Ooga Booga Land](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/oogaboogaland)** — A playable community world connecting our projects, contributors, games, and public Bitcoin data.
- **[Bedrock](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bedrock)** — Our homepage and the story behind the tribe.
- **[Banana Pay Server](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananapayserver)** — Payment integration and event feeds supporting Ooga Booga Land.
- **[Banana Certified Accounting](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananacertifiedaccounting)** — Our funding plans, budgets, and financial documentation.

Our software is available to inspect, use, adapt, and share under each repository's license. Check each project's status and safety guidance before using it.

## Join the tribe

Start with a project's README and contribution guidance. Ask questions, improve documentation, report bugs, test changes, or propose an idea. Experience is welcome; curiosity is enough to begin.

## Supporting the work

Contributors currently volunteer their time. Our initial funding goal is to cover project expenses, especially AI development tools. We are exploring fiscal sponsorship and documenting our plans in [Banana Certified Accounting](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananacertifiedaccounting). A tax-deductible donation arrangement has not yet been confirmed.
