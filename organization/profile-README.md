# Team Ooga Booga 🪨 🔥 🎲 🍌

We eat bananas. We make code. We give code to the tribe.

Team Ooga Booga is a worldwide community of volunteer developers. We build in public so people can follow the work, get interested, and learn how to contribute. We actively teach people who want to get involved and help them take part themselves. We use AI tools as part of our development process and share what we learn.

We call it **gorilla open source**: bring an idea, build in public, and help the next person get started.

[Visit our homepage](https://oogabooga.rocks)

## Our caves

- **[EntropyLab](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/entropylab)** — An offline, air-gapped Bitcoin key and wallet calculator for exploration, testing, and learning.
- **[Lightning Foundry](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/lightningfoundry)** — Pre-alpha design and tooling for operating, automating, and studying Lightning routing nodes. The team's launched node does not establish a working Foundry deployment.
- **[Ooga Booga Land](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/oogaboogaland)** — A playable community world connecting our projects, contributors, games, and public Bitcoin data.
- **[Bedrock](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bedrock)** — Our homepage and the story behind the tribe.
- **[Banana Pay Server](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananapayserver)** — Payment integration and event feeds supporting Ooga Booga Land.
- **[Banana Certified Accounting](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananacertifiedaccounting)** — Our funding plans, budgets, proposed expense rules, and public financial updates, with general-purpose open-source accounting software planned for anyone to use, the team included. No accounting application exists here yet.
- **[Ooga Arcade](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/oogaarcade)** — A planned standalone home for Ooga Booga Land's games. The repository is a placeholder; extraction has not started.
- **[Oogatron](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/oogatron)** — Contributor analytics and jumbotron tooling for the community world. Its repository has no license file in the reviewed snapshot; reuse terms need clarification.

Check each repository's license and contribution guidance before reusing its work. A public repository alone does not establish reuse terms. Projects are young and move quickly, so check their status and safety guidance before relying on them, especially anything that touches keys or funds.

## Join the tribe

Start with a project's README and contribution guidance. Ask questions, improve documentation, report bugs, test changes, or propose an idea through the project's issues. Experience is welcome; curiosity is enough to begin.

## Supporting the work

Contributors volunteer their time. Our immediate priorities are preparing bitcoin donations and reducing project AI costs. Our Lightning node has launched, and we plan to accept bitcoin donations from individual supporters and other interested parties, with the treasury held in bitcoin. Donation instructions and custody arrangements are still being prepared. No sponsor has accepted us and no U.S. charitable-giving arrangement is established; SPI contact and application are deferred. Our plans, estimates, and future financial reports are public in [Banana Certified Accounting](https://github.com/OogaBoogaX/bananacertifiedaccounting).

The best support today is contributing, testing, and sharing the projects.

Take fire. Make fire. Share fire.
